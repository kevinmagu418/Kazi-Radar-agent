# KaziRadar Keyword Strategy

## Primary Keywords
*High volume, broad intent.*

- **AI opportunity scanner**: For users looking for AI-driven discovery.
- **opportunity discovery platform**: For general opportunity seekers.
- **remote jobs Africa**: Targeting the growing remote work market in the continent.
- **startup funding opportunities**: For entrepreneurs and founders.
- **grant discovery platform**: For NGOs, researchers, and students.
- **entrepreneurship opportunities**: For aspiring business owners.
- **AI job discovery**: For modern job seekers looking for an edge.

## Secondary Keywords
*Niche, specific intent.*

- **AI scout**: Brand-aligned term for automated discovery.
- **opportunity intelligence**: Professional tier discovery.
- **funding intelligence**: Specialized for startups/investors.
- **grant intelligence**: Specialized for academic/non-profit.
- **startup scouting**: For investors or ecosystem builders.
- **opportunity finder**: Simple, high-intent search term.
- **remote opportunity platform**: Focus on remote-first lifestyle.

## Local SEO Keywords (Kenya & Africa Focus)
*Geographic targeting.*

- **jobs in Kenya**: Primary local traffic driver.
- **grants in Kenya**: Localized funding intent.
- **startup funding Kenya**: Specifically for the Silicon Savannah.
- **entrepreneurship Kenya**: Local ecosystem growth.
- **agriculture funding Africa**: Sector-specific high-value keyword.
- **fintech opportunities Africa**: Sector-specific high-growth keyword.

## Content Pillars & Semantic Clusters

### 1. The "Speed" Pillar
- *Keywords:* Faster discovery, real-time alerts, priority signals, competitive edge.
- *Goal:* Position KaziRadar as the "Speed Force" of opportunity discovery.

### 2. The "Intelligence" Pillar
- *Keywords:* AI-powered, neural matching, precision scouting, intelligence engine.
- *Goal:* Establish technical authority and trust in the AI's matching capabilities.

### 3. The "Discovery" Pillar
- *Keywords:* Uncovering hidden gems, private tender lists, startup boards, global ingestion.
- *Goal:* Highlight the breadth and depth of sources.
